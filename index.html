📖About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BrainSprout Kids (“the app”, “we”, “us”), made by Mehdi Technologies, handles information. The app is a quiz game designed for children, and we built it so that it does not collect, store or share any personal information.

📱Information stored on the device

To make the game work, the app saves a small amount of information only on the device it is installed on:

  • The first name or nickname the player types in (used to say “Great job, Sam!”)
  • Coins earned, best scores and the chosen difficulty mode
  • Unfinished quizzes, so the player can continue later

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive it, and we cannot see it. We recommend using a nickname instead of a full name.

🚫Information we do not collect

  • No email addresses, phone numbers or home addresses
  • No location data
  • No photos, contacts, camera or microphone access
  • No advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools
  • No in-app purchases

🧒Children’s privacy

BrainSprout Kids is made for children. Because the app does not collect personal information, it is designed to meet the requirements of children’s privacy laws such as the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play’s Families Policy.

The app shows no advertising, has no chat or social features, and contains no links that take children out of the app.

🔗Third-party services

The app does not share information with third parties. It is built with the open-source Expo and React Native frameworks, which we have set up without analytics or advertising.

When the app is downloaded from an app store (such as Google Play), that store may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from the store.

🗑️Deleting your data

All game data can be removed at any time by either:

  • Opening the app → Profile → Reset all progress, or
  • Uninstalling the app, which deletes everything it saved

🔒Security

Game data is kept in the app’s private storage on the device, which other apps cannot read. Because nothing is sent over the internet, there is no data for us to lose or leak.

🔄Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. If a change ever involves collecting new information, we will update the app and this policy before that change happens.
